# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Patients and hackathon judges using a fictional outpatient booking demo.

## Product Purpose

APOLLO coordinates outpatient questions and bookings through controlled AI agents, MCP tools, and authoritative operational services.

## Positioning

It makes the existing agentic booking path visible without exposing infrastructure or allowing browser-side access to protected services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The web UI uses backend-supplied treatments, availability, billing, policy answers, safety results, notifications, and traces. Demo data is fictional.
